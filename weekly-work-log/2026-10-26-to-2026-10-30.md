3. TimescaleDB internal chunks depend on their parent hypertable metadata.

5. TimescaleDB restoration requires appropriate pre-restore and post-restore handling.
6. Docker container names and image names are separate.
7. Flyway migration ordering and baseline configuration affect migration execution.
